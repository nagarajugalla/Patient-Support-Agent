Global Healthcare Provider: Introducing an Agentic Solution for Patient Support and Compliance

● Company: A multinational healthcare provider serving millions of patients worldwide. They balance high patient service demand with strict regulatory oversight, making consistency, compliance, and cost effi ciency critical. 

● Scenario: Patient support is currently handled entirely by human agents, leading to high operational costs and inconsistent information delivery. The organization faces challenges in quickly providing up-to-date and compliant information, especially regarding insurance benefi ts and appointment scheduling. The lack of effi cient data integration across disparate patient record systems further complicates personalized support eff orts. 

● Challenge: Build and launch a secure and compliant Agentforce "Patient Support Agent" to streamline common patient interactions and ensure consistent, accurate, and up-to-date information delivery, leading to signifi cant cost savings and improved patient trust. 

● Short-term Build & Deployment Plan: Outline a plan to build and launch the "Patient Support Agent". The customer’s stated goal is to reduce inbound calls related to common FAQs (insurance benefi ts, appointment procedures) by 15–20% in the fi rst quarter, while ensuring full compliance with regulatory requirements. The new agent must securely integrate with one primary patient record system for basic personalized information and demonstrate a measurable reduction in manual agent escalations, improving operational effi ciency and patient trust. 

● Long-term Agentic Roadmap: Develop a roadmap for the agent to become a "Personal Health Navigator," expanding to proactive health reminders, prescription refi ll management, integration with all patient data systems, and guidance through complex care pathways, all while maintaining rigorous security and compliance standards. This will ultimately better patient engagement and reduce preventable readmissions.
